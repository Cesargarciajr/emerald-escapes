Our story

We're a small team of Galway locals who think Ireland is best seen slowly.

Emerald Escapes began in 2026 when two friends — a tour guide from Connemara and a chef from Cork — started taking visiting friends to the places guidebooks forget: the pub with the Tuesday-night session, the cove only the fishermen use, the cottage with the best view of the Twelve Bens.

Today we design small-group and self-guided trips for travellers who want more than a checklist. Every experience is tested by our team, supports local businesses and comes with a real person on the other end of the phone.
